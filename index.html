    Process Improvement

    AI-generated recommendations to streamline operations across all three studios

    High Impact

    Connect Google Calendar for Automated Scheduling

    Your team spends approximately 6 hours per week manually coordinating instructor schedules across three locations. Connecting Google Calendar would enable AI to auto-assign instructors based on availability, student preferences, and travel time between studios.

    Projected Impact: $14,400 per year saved, 25% fewer scheduling conflicts
    High Impact

    Implement SMS Follow-Up via Twilio Integration

    Text messages achieve a 98% open rate versus 32% for email. Adding SMS to your Speed to Lead and Follow-Up sequences would significantly increase response rates, with new leads receiving an instant personalized text within seconds of their inquiry.

    Projected Impact: 40% higher lead conversion, approximately $8,000 additional monthly revenue
    Medium Impact

    Add Stripe or Square for Automated Billing

    Automated billing with dunning (failed payment recovery) could recover an estimated 15 to 20 students per month who lapse due to payment issues rather than actual disinterest in continuing their dance education.

    Projected Impact: $6,000 to $9,000 per month in recovered revenue
    Medium Impact

    Create Student Progress Dashboard

    Students who can visualize their progress stay enrolled 2.3 times longer. A student-facing progress tracker showing dance styles learned, hours logged, and milestones reached would boost retention and create shareable moments for social media.

    Projected Impact: 30% improvement in student retention
    Quick Win

    Automate Review Requests After Milestone Lessons

    After a student completes their 5th, 10th, and 25th lesson, automatically send a personalized review request to Google and Yelp. Studios with more reviews rank higher in local search and attract more organic leads at zero cost.

    Projected Impact: 3 to 5 times more Google reviews, higher local search ranking
