Marcus & Priya,

Thank you for reaching out to us about our wedding dance lessons at the Arthur Murray Dance Center in Castle Rock. We specialize in wedding preparation and can help you get ready for your special day at the Stanley Hotel in Estes Park.

We offer two new student packages to choose from. The second option is ideal for couples who want to learn their first dance, it includes choreography. The first option is a social dance program designed to teach you essential social dance skills.

Option 1: The Introductory Offer is $95 and includes:

  • Two 30-minute personal lessons
  • Two group classes
  • Two practice parties

The personal lessons in the introductory offer are 30 minutes long and can be scheduled between 1:00 pm and 9:00 pm, Monday through Friday. Your instructor will teach you basic social dances in a fun and easy-to-understand manner. The group classes and practice parties provide an opportunity to learn other social dances for your reception and help you progress faster. This program is designed for students who want to learn how to social dance and is not recommended for those who want to learn a specific dance to a particular song, like your special song ‘At Last’ by Etta James.

Option 2: Our wedding lessons are $99 per lesson for up to 10 personal lessons. The personal lessons in the wedding package are 40 minutes long and can also be scheduled between 1:00 pm and 9:00 pm, Monday through Friday. We typically recommend three lessons to create a choreographed routine with an entrance, and a dip or an exit. Your instructor will work with you to select a dance that complements your song choice and can choreograph something special for your first dance.

We can also help you with a mother-son dance for you and your mom, which is a wonderful way to make your reception even more special.

To begin, simply set up your initial Personal Lesson and let us know which package best suits your needs. Pick whichever way is easiest: call 303-814-8196, text 303-351-2656, or email nicki@coloradodancestudios.com.
