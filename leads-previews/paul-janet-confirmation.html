Here's a sneak peek at what to expect during your first dance class.

Finding Us

We're at 1057 Park Street, Unit C in Castle Rock. As you enter the parking lot, look straight ahead and to the right of the 105W brewery — our sign is directly over the studio entrance. If you need any help finding us, give us a call.

What To Wear

Business casual is great — but comfort is key, so jeans or workout attire are perfectly fine too. For shoes we recommend a low heel that stays on your foot, or flats for Janet. Please avoid rubber soles — they stick to the floor and hinder movement.

What Happens When You Arrive
1. A warm welcome and a brief tour of the studio.
2. A few quick pieces of paperwork.
3. A short chat to build a custom lesson plan around your goals.
4. Meet your instructor and start dancing — 6:30–7:00 PM.
Need To Reschedule?

We have a 12-hour cancellation policy. Late cancellations are subject to a $35 fee to cover the instructor's time. Please let us know as soon as you can if plans change.

If you have any questions before your lesson, give me a call or reply to this email. We look forward to meeting you.
