Your $95 New Student Offer includes:

  • Two 30-minute Personal Lessons with an instructor

Personal Lessons are taught Monday through Friday between 1:00 and 9:00 PM. Your instructor will guide you both through a variety of basic social dances in a fun, easy-to-follow way — no experience needed at all. The group classes and parties are how you progress faster, practice what you've learned, and meet our wonderful community of students.

To answer your question about what to bring — comfort is key. Business casual is great, but jeans or workout attire are perfectly fine too. For shoes, we recommend a low heel that stays on your foot, or flats — and please avoid rubber soles, as they tend to stick to the floor and make movement harder.
